How I Scale
Meta Ads

The system behind the results, the same one I run every week.

1

Know the Real Numbers

Break-even ROAS, cash-on-delivery returns and profit per order come first, so every campaign is judged on delivered sales, not on Ads Manager alone.

2

Build Real Creative Variety

Five to seven genuinely different ads per ad set: different angle, format, hook and proof. Since Andromeda, the creative does the targeting.

3

Keep the Budget Together

One campaign, one ad set, so the algorithm learns faster instead of splitting money across many small tests.

4

Hands Off for 3 Days

No budget, audience or ad changes while the system learns. Decisions made on one day of data kill good ads.

5

Cut and Replace

After day 3, clear losers are switched off and replaced with new ideas, not copies of the winner.

6

Scale in Steps

Winning campaigns grow 20% at a time, and duplicates take the bigger jumps without resetting what already works.
